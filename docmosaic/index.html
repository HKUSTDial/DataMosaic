01. THE ENVIRONMENT

Multimodal Document Environment

One document. Multiple views. Every detail connected.

DocMosaic connects the big picture with the details that matter. Document overviews, page thumbnails, original page images, and structured elements share the same document and page identifiers, so agents can move from a global view to precise evidence.

  • See the whole documentScan numbered page thumbnails in a compact overview to find promising pages.
  • Look closerSwitch from lightweight thumbnails to original page images for visual inspection.
  • Access the elementsRead text, tables, figures, and formulas with their layout and source locations preserved.

02. THE FRAMEWORK

Agentic Orchestration
Framework

One question. The right agents. A connected investigation.

DocMosaic breaks a document task into focused steps. A coordinating agent chooses what to inspect next, delegates to specialized tools, and refines the workflow as evidence arrives.

  • Navigate & retrieveLocate relevant pages, passages, tables, and figures.
  • Inspect & computeRead visual details and calculate from collected evidence.
  • Check & refineAssess what is known and continue searching when evidence is incomplete.

03. THE EXPERIENCE

Interaction Workflow

Ask naturally. Explore visually. Stay close to the source.

Bring your documents into a shared reading space. Follow the agent’s investigation, inspect its supporting evidence, and move between an answer and the page that supports it.

  • Ask across documentsInvestigate questions that span pages and files.
  • Follow the investigationSee the steps and tools used to gather evidence.
  • Return to the sourceOpen cited pages and inspect the relevant content in context.

04. THE LEARNING PATH

From LLM to SLM

Let capable models teach. Let compact models take the next step.

Use strong multimodal models to produce document-solving trajectories, then train smaller models on individual abilities and complete workflows. Keep the connection between the question, the action, and its evidence.

  • Collect expert trajectoriesCapture tool use, document navigation, and evidence-grounded reasoning.
  • Train focused capabilitiesLearn individual document skills and end-to-end task execution.
  • Serve compact modelsDeploy trained models through vLLM for a self-hosted document agent.
